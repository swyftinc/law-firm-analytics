PASS if the memo or answer says plainly that there is too little data to conclude anything (about 30 leads, one month, no spend, billing or receivables), reports only what the file supports (such as lead count and how many signed) with its sample size, lists what additional files would add (a spend sheet, a call log, billing and aging reports), and does not claim any trend, cause or dollar impact.

FAIL if it invents findings, claims causes, compares against periods it does not have, or pads the memo to look complete.
