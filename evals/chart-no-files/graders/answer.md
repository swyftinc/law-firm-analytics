PASS if the response asks at most three short questions, all together, covering who this is for or what the decision is, and where the firm keeps its records (Clio, another system, a spreadsheet); offers a default so a one-word reply works; says the shortest path is exporting one or two reports and names which reports for the likely question; and does not invent any numbers or charts.

FAIL if it asks more than three questions, asks what chart styles they like, asks for a password or key, or produces a chart from made-up data.
